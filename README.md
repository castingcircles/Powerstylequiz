**What's Your Power Style? — Social Discipline Window Quiz**

An interactive self-assessment quiz built for [Casting Circles LLC](https://www.castingcircles.com).

This quiz explores how you use power across relationships — as a parent, teacher, partner, manager, or helper — and plots your result on the Social Discipline Window across two axes: support and limit.
